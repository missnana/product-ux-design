About

Hi, I'm Nadine.

Soft Interactions is more than a portfolio name to me. It's an attitude.

I'm a UX specialist with a soft spot for clear structures, quiet details and digital experiences that feel good for everyone. I work where design, psychology and technology meet. Empathy is my strongest tool: in research, in user flows, in defining interactions and in working with stakeholders and teammates.

  • An ear for language, hierarchy and flow
  • Curiosity about real users and their contexts
  • Joy in understanding problems and solving them elegantly
  • A weakness for pastel colours, clear user flows and a pinch of common sense
