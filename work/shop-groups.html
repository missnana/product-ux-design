Navigation, shop groups & product pages, rebuilt from scratch

Restructuring how people find their photo product at Pixum, starting with the navigation. Every step was a hypothesis, and most of them were A/B tested.

Company
Pixum, online shop for photo products
Role
UX/UI Designer
Team
Product team, web analytics, content
Timeline
2018–2023, web on desktop & mobile
≈ +13 %Uplift after the shop group relaunch, statistically significant
≈ +30 %Conversion with the new product cards
≈ +15 %Conversion on desktop with a filter grid for photo gifts
01 · Situation

What happened

Pixum's shop groups, the category pages for photo books, calendars, gifts and more, had grown for years on an old CMS. The pages weren't responsive, the main navigation was getting crowded, and the way from category to product to the design editor was long.

02 · Approach

What I did

Together with the product team I rebuilt the structure step by step on the new CMS: first the navigation, then the shop groups, then the product pages below them. Each change started as a hypothesis with a clear test goal.

  • Navigation firstThe main navigation had to work on every screen size, crowded menus got a fallback, and products like photo puzzles moved to where people look for them.
  • Shop groups and product pages from scratchNew shop group pages with a clearer key visual, the full range of formats right below it and a direct way into the design editor, plus responsive product pages underneath.
  • Product cards that help people decideBetter product images, choice chips, readable prices and a clear CTA, tested against a filter grid. On mobile, a two-column grid for quicker comparison.
03 · Result

What it changed

The relaunched shop group pages raised conversion, cart value and revenue per session, by about 13 % overall. The new product cards lifted conversion by around 30 %. Not everything worked: moving photo puzzles into the main navigation shifted the clicks but not the sales, and the filter grid helped on desktop but not on mobile. That's exactly why every step was tested.
