01 · Situation

What happened

Every company I worked with started from the same place: an inconsistent look across products, layouts that weren't fully responsive, and engineers building the same element again and again in slightly different colours and variants. Nothing scaled as requirements grew.

02 · Approach

What I did

I built design systems together with engineering, as the base for efficient development and a clean, consistent brand across the whole company.

  • Start from what's really thereWe broke every existing design down into atoms, components and templates, following Atomic Design, before adding anything new.
  • Build the foundation with engineeringDesign and development agreed on the system together, so components in Figma match components in code and Storybook.
  • Own the system, improve it step by stepInstead of bending a framework like Bootstrap to fit the brand, we built our own system for our business needs and refined it iteratively. Even accessibility can be added later when the structure is sound.
03 · Result

What it changed

The design systems I built made it simpler and more efficient for everyone to implement designs. Design changes, for example from marketing, can be rolled out quickly and consistently. And it doesn't take forever: a focused start and at least one engineer who loves design are enough to get going.
