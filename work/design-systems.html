01 · Situation

What happened

Every company I worked with started from the same place: an inconsistent look across products, layouts that weren't fully responsive, and engineers building the same element again and again in slightly different colours and variants. Nothing scaled as requirements grew. At DYN there was no design system yet, only design decisions spread across products.

02 · Approach

What I did

At Pixum and reCup I built the systems together with UI designers and engineering. At DYN I'm building it on my own, with engineering and AI.

  • Pixum: an in-house system from scratchBuilt in Figma as the foundation for web, app and software: existing designs broken down into atoms, components and templates following Atomic Design, then agreed with engineering.
  • reCup: growing the system with the platformDeveloped the existing design system further alongside the B2B platform.
  • DYN: multi-brand with sport modesCollecting existing design decisions and turning them into one system for several brands, B2B and B2C, with sport modes for the special needs of streaming and different sports.
  • AI as part of the workflowAt DYN the system is built in cooperation with AI, between design and code.
03 · Result

What it changed

The design systems made it simpler and more efficient for everyone to implement designs, and design changes can be rolled out quickly and consistently. Instead of bending a framework like Bootstrap to fit the brand, as was common around 2014, we build systems around our own business needs and improve them step by step. Even accessibility can be added later when the structure is sound.
