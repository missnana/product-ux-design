← All work Case study · Mein Pixum

An account area customers can manage themselves

Rebuilding Mein Pixum, the customer account, from creating an account and resetting a password to the dashboard, so customers find what they need and trust what they see.

Company
Pixum, online shop for photo products
Role
UX/UI Designer
Team
Product teams, web analytics
Timeline
2021–2022, web on desktop & mobile
Fewer resetsFewer customers needed a second password reset on the same day
More secureChanging the email address now needs the account password
Live in 2022New dashboard and account pages, live since October 2022
01 · Situation

What happened

Mein Pixum, where customers see their orders, photos and personal data, had grown without a plan. The navigation didn't reflect what people actually looked for, "My photos" and "My albums" looked the same to customers, and many couldn't reset their password on their own, which led to drop-offs in checkout and support tickets.

02 · Approach

What I did

I analysed how customers really used the account area and rebuilt it step by step, each change written as a hypothesis.

  • Navigation built on real usageThe dashboard shows the most-clicked pages, a dropdown leads straight into each area, and "My photos" and "My albums" became one.
  • Getting in without frictionCreating an account needs fewer confirmation emails, and the password reset was rebuilt so more customers succeed on their own.
  • Trust by designChanging the email address needs the password, the salutation became optional, and the dashboard greets customers personally.
03 · Result

What it changed

Fewer customers needed a second password reset on the same day, a sign that the new flow works the first time. The new dashboard and account pages went live in October 2022. Measuring more was hard: tracking in the account area was limited, so several changes were based on web analytics rather than A/B tests.
